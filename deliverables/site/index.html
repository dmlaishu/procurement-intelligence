Procurement intelligence

LabProcure

LabProcure helps you plan purchases of laboratory and diagnostic equipment in World Bank health projects. Choose Kenya or India, the equipment and your budget, and it estimates when the contract will be awarded, based on similar past tenders. You can also ask the AI Assistant about suppliers, prices, risks and lessons. Every answer shows the record it came from, and where the public data has nothing, it says so.
