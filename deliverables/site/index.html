How the dates are made

Three dates. Three different promises.

An interviewer, an auditor or a procurement officer will ask where a date comes from. Each one has its own basis and its own level of confidence.

Model

Likely award (P50)

A gradient-boosted quantile model trained on 759 World Bank tenders opened before January 2024 and tested on 259 opened after. Half of comparable tenders are awarded sooner than this.

Model

Conservative planning date (P80)

The 80th-percentile model. On the later tenders, actual award came by this date 76% of the time (80% is the target). This is the date to put in a plan.

Assumption

Delivery

The P80 award date plus the median contracted delivery period from award notices. Actual delivery dates are not published, so this is an assumption, not a validated prediction.
