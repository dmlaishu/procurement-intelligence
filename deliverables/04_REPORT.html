1. Summary

World Bank health projects buy a large amount of laboratory and diagnostic equipment, but the records of what was bought, how and why it worked sit in separate places: contract award data, procurement notices, and PDF reports. We built a working prototype that connects them for this one domain, across every country. It turns them into answers a procurement team can use, through a cited chatbot and package briefs.

Question Answer from the prototype
How big is the domain in public data? 2,220 contracts worth US$1,375.4M across 91 countries, 179 projects and 1,277 suppliers, found by classifying 17,058 health-goods contract descriptions in four languages
Can the silos be joined? Yes. 100% of contracts link to their project and 67% to their tender; 131 project reports connect through Project ID (115,327 graph links)
What does the market look like? Diagnostic imaging is the largest category (US$451.6M). 53% of value was signed in 2020-2022 (COVID-19). Domestic suppliers took 58% of value; 16 suppliers won in two or more countries
How long do tenders take? Medians from invitation to award range from 90 days (Laboratory analyzers) to 183 days (Biosafety, sterilization and cold chain), censoring-aware
Is the forecast better than a benchmark? Yes. Out-of-time median error 47 days vs 56 for cohort benchmarks (17% better)
Does the chatbot answer correctly? It routed 100% of 16 labelled questions to the right tool; ranked and aggregate answers matched an independent query in 6 of 6 checks; 100% of statements cited
Can hallucination be controlled? Yes, by design. 100% of 360 brief statements cite evidence; the verifier blocked 124 of 125 deliberately injected false statements
Are the risk models ready to drive decisions? Not yet. Single-bid AUC 0.67; re-tender skipped: not enough positives; award amendment AUC 0.55. Use as indicators only
Can users ask their own questions of a country's documents? Yes. A document Q&A answers from Kenya, India, Nigeria project documents (111 documents, 4,761 passages) with country and category menus; 10/10 evaluation questions passed and every source came from the selected country. A new country is one command (§7)
Does it run on the target platform? Yes. All notebooks ran end to end on Databricks (2026-10-27) with results matching the local prototype (§5.5)

3. Data: how the domain was assembled

Step Result
Health-practice Goods contracts pulled (FY2015+) 17,058
Classified as laboratory or diagnostic equipment 2,220 contracts, US$1,375.4M
Projects that bought such equipment 179 (all their 14,613 health-goods contracts kept as market context)
Procurement notices of those projects (Goods) 15,158 → 10,403 tenders (1,947 for lab and diagnostic items)
Project documents parsed (PAD, ISR, ICR, IEG review, procurement plan) 131 → 6,187 section-aware passages
Suppliers 7,835 raw names → 7,540 resolved (94 fuzzy merges)

Data quality found along the way.

  • Language and encoding. Descriptions come in English, French, Spanish and Portuguese, and accents often arrive garbled ("r?actifs"). Patterns therefore tolerate a wildcard in place of accents.
  • Uninformative descriptions. Some contracts are described only as "Lot 3", "Item 42" or "no contract". They stay unclassified rather than being guessed.
  • Missing invitation notices. Many emergency purchases (direct contracting, UN agencies) have no invitation notice. They count in market statistics but cannot give a tender duration. Only 1,068 tenders have both an invitation and an award; 122 are still open (censored) and 442 had no award after two years.
  • No completion reports yet. Most COVID-era projects are still active, so lessons come mainly from older, closed health projects (150 lessons extracted).

Conflicting evidence. 34 projects have dated ratings from several sources (ISR supervision, the Bank's ICR, IEG's independent review). 7. Document Q&A by country

Users pick a country and a category (ten equipment types, or the procurement process) and ask their own question; the answer is built only from that country's World Bank project documents and lists its sources (document type, title, project, page, link).

Step What happens
Fetch projects that bought lab equipment in the country, lab-related health projects from the Projects API, and pinned regional programmes; for each, the newest appraisal document, project papers, procurement plans, supervision report, completion report and IEG reviews, stored as <country>/<document type>/<id>.pdf
Process text per page, section-aware passages that keep section and pages, tagged with the categories they mention
Index embeddings in a vector database (ChromaDB locally, Databricks Vector Search on the platform), filtered by country and category
Answer hybrid retrieval (meaning + exact words), then the sentences that answer the question, each with its source; or a short written summary that is shown only if every citation and number is in the cited sources
Country Projects Documents Passages
Kenya 8 29 1,239
India 12 55 2,556
Nigeria 6 27 966

Evaluation: 10 questions with a known correct source (a project, document type or phrase) and one the documents cannot answer. 10/10 passed: 9 answered with the expected source in the top five, 1 correctly declined, every source from the selected country, about 1.4 s per answer locally and about 3 s on Databricks. Adding a country does not rebuild the others: Nigeria was added with one command in under four minutes, and Kenya and India were not re-embedded.

Answers are sentences quoted from the documents, so they are only as specific as the documents: TB projects in India describe "newer and quicker diagnostic equipment (including … rapid technology)" rather than naming machines.

9. Limitations

Limitation Effect What would fix it
Public data only no bid prices per unit, technical evaluation reports, service contracts or equipment downtime Phase 2: STEP procurement records, asset registers, maintenance logs
Keyword taxonomy a residue of contracts stays "Other goods"; a few generic items are misclassified labelled set of about 500 descriptions; LLM classifier for the residue
Few completion reports for COVID-era projects lessons skew to older projects add IEG thematic evaluations and the next ICR cohort as they publish
Rule-based lesson extraction locally lessons limited to numbered paragraphs in Lessons / Key-factor sections the Databricks path uses LLM extraction with a quote check (733 lessons)
Thin risk-model labels single-bid, re-tender and amendment models are indicative only more countries and internal records; recalibration
Verifier misses a fabricated number that appears elsewhere in the evidence 1 of 125 injected errors can pass number-unit binding + entailment judge
Learning loop run once retraining and drift triggers work, but have not yet run over weeks of new data un-pause the proc_lab_pipeline job schedule
Document Q&A quotes rather than summarises answers read as quotations; a vague question gets a "not found" written-summary mode (checked); specific questions
Documents chosen by project country studies and documents not linked to a project are not fetched add a keyword search of the Documents API per country
Free hosting limits the public chatbot sleeps after a few days idle; the Databricks app needs a workspace sign-in; the first Vector Search build took about 1.5 hours on the free tier paid tier or provisioned throughput

10. Recommendations and next steps

  1. Pilot the chatbot with procurement specialists and biomedical engineers in two or three health programmes that are about to buy lab equipment. Measure plan-preparation time and evidence usefulness.
  2. Add the equipment's life cycle: service and maintenance contracts, reagent purchases after equipment purchases, and asset registers. These answer the domain's real question, whether the equipment was still working a year later.
  3. Build a labelled taxonomy set and an evaluation set of about 200 question-answer pairs with the domain experts.
  4. Switch on the learning loop on Databricks (un-pause the weekly job) and add the next countries to the document Q&A (add_country in notebook 30, then the proc_rag_pipeline job).
  5. Extend the scope configuration to the next domain (e.g. medicines, or civil works). The pipeline already runs on an India roads scope and a three-country scope with no code changes.
